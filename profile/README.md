# Tuteliq

**Child-safety deep tech. Proprietary AI models that detect grooming, bullying, self-harm, fraud and exploitation, before a child is harmed.**

[Website](https://tuteliq.ai) · [Documentation](https://docs.tuteliq.ai) · [Dashboard](https://tuteliq.ai/dashboard) · [MCP server](https://docs.tuteliq.ai/mcp-server)

</div>

---

Tuteliq researches, trains and runs its own child-safety models, developed with criminologists and child-protection specialists. Detection is behavioural, not keyword-based: it reads intent, context and how a conversation escalates over time, across text, voice, images and video in 32 languages.

Use it the way that fits your platform:

- **Detection API.** One call per check: grooming, bullying, self-harm, sextortion, romance scams, social engineering, radicalisation, synthetic media and more, with age-calibrated risk scores, rationale and recommended actions.
- **Licensed models.** Tuteliq's models in a dedicated or self-hosted deployment. Contact [sales@tuteliq.ai](mailto:sales@tuteliq.ai).
- **Moderation platform.** An optional incident queue with triage, human and AI agent review, and a signed audit receipt for every decision, in the [dashboard](https://tuteliq.ai/dashboard), over the API or through MCP.
- **Age and identity verification.** Document checks, passport chip reads, liveness and face matching.

**Privacy by design:** no user content is ever stored. Incident records hold only the model's assessment, encrypted at rest or end-to-end encrypted with your own key, and can be turned off entirely.
